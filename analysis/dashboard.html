Read the results carefully

Six-month retention clears its target; senior-hire retention falls short. Regretted turnover is below its ceiling under both known-classification scenarios.

These are descriptive results from synthetic data. Economic indicators provide context; this dashboard does not establish why employees leave.

Turnover covers 1 January–31 December 2025. Its denominator is average daily headcount, not the number of employees contributing during the year.

Retention context uses hire date. Turnover context uses the start of its window. Same-day releases and stale observations are excluded.

Sources, lineage and methodology

Reporting source: reconciled SQLite views. Rates are recomputed from counts when filters change; percentages are never averaged.

Employee classifications follow the documented inclusive termination-date boundary, calendar-month anniversaries, maturity rules and metric-specific exclusions.

External providers: Eurostat unemployment and vacancy releases; World Bank CPI archives. CPI archive months are mapped to reviewed WDI update dates, not claimed as first national publication dates.

Detailed local evidence: analysis/sql_reporting.json, analysis/metric_external_coverage.json, analysis/*_external_lineage.json and docs/metric-and-quality-rules.md.

Generated from saved evidence by the offline pipeline. No external libraries, fonts, analytics or network requests. Displayed percentages are rounded; calculations use full precision.
